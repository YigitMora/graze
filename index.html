SHEER

Support

Sheer is a single-player arcade game for iPhone. You leave a cliff in a wingsuit and fly down a mountain. The closer you fly to the rock, the faster your score climbs — but nothing counts until you carry it through a saddle and land. Crash, and everything you have not banked goes with you.

GETTING HELP

Questions, bug reports and feedback go here:

  • github.com/YigitMora/graze/issues — open an issue. A GitHub account is required, and issues are public.

When reporting a problem, the most useful things to include are your device model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

WHAT THE APP DOES NOT DO

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics and no tracking.
  • No network access at all — the game works entirely offline.

YOUR DATA

Sheer collects nothing. Your best run, your progress and your two sound settings stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Hold to dive: nose down, faster, losing height.
  • Let go to flare: more lift, slower descent, away from the rock.
  • Flying close earns fast. Flying close costs height, and you need height to reach the next saddle.
  • Passing a saddle banks what you have earned. Reaching the landing zone banks the rest.
  • Banked score earns credit. Credit buys suits, a higher exit, and new mountains.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored.
